My Nature Explorer Page

Discover the beauty of forests, rivers, mountains, and wildlife.

Forests

Forests are home to many plants and animals. They produce oxygen, absorb carbon dioxide, and help maintain ecological balance.

Rivers

Rivers provide fresh water, support agriculture, and create habitats for fish and other aquatic life.

Mountains

Mountains offer breathtaking landscapes, influence weather patterns, and are home to unique wildlife.

Wildlife

Wildlife includes all animals living in their natural habitats. Protecting wildlife helps preserve biodiversity.
