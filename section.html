Facts about dogs

Dogs have a sense of smell that is hundreds of times more sensitive than humans. It has been proven that dogs can detect certain diseases, such as cancer and diabetes, through their sense of smell. Dogs are also known for their loyalty and companionship, making them one of the most popular pets worldwide.

Services given by dogs

Dogs provide various services, including assistance for people with disabilities, search and rescue operations, and therapy support. They also help with security known to be effective at guarding property. They are also highly intelligent animals and can be trained to perform a wide range of tasks.
